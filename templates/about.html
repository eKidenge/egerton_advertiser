About the Publication

The Egerton Advertiser is a publication of Egerton Green Movement Network (EGMN) based in Njoro, Egerton, Nakuru County, Kenya. As a news magazine dedicated towards uniting and educating society, and empowering business ventures at grassroots level, we carry the weight of responsibility that comes with being a trusted source of information for our community and beyond.

Our publication is the Official Gazette on Environment, Society, and Business, dedicated to covering climate action, sustainability, conservation, social development, and business empowerment in Kenya and beyond.

We are dedicated to informing society, empowering business, and upholding the highest standards of truth, integrity, and service in all our work.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission

To inform society and empower business through accurate, timely, and trustworthy reporting on environment, society, and sustainable development. We are committed to uniting and educating communities, and fostering grassroots business ventures that drive economic growth and social progress.

Vision

To be the most trusted and authoritative source of news on environment, society, and business in Kenya. We envision a nation where every citizen has access to reliable, impartial, and high-quality information that empowers informed decision-making, sustainable development, and business success.

Core Values

Value 01
Environmental Stewardship

We are committed to protecting and preserving our environment. Our reporting promotes sustainable practices, conservation, and responsible resource management for future generations.

Value 02
Social Justice

We stand for justice, equity, and fairness in all our reporting. We give voice to the voiceless and advocate for human rights, social development, and community empowerment.

Value 03
Truth & Integrity

We are uncompromising in our commitment to truth and accuracy. Our journalism is built on a foundation of honesty, transparency, and ethical conduct.

Value 04
Community Service

We serve our community with dedication and integrity. Our work is driven by a commitment to the public good, social development, and the betterment of society.

Value 05
Sustainability

We promote sustainable development and responsible resource use. Our journalism supports a sustainable future for Kenya and beyond, encouraging responsible practices.

Value 06
Excellence

We strive for excellence in everything we do. From reporting to design, we aim to be the best in class and set the standard for quality journalism in Kenya.
