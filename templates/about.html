ENVIRONMENT & SOCIETY

"Preserving Nature, Building Communities"

Established 2024

Our Mission

ENVIRONMENT & SOCIETY is committed to delivering accurate, timely, and trustworthy reporting on environmental and social issues that shape our world. We believe in the power of journalism to inform, educate, and empower communities to take action on climate change, sustainability, and social justice.

We are dedicated to upholding the highest standards of journalistic integrity, ensuring that every story we tell is fact-checked, balanced, and fair. We serve as the Official Gazette on Environment and Society, carrying the weight of responsibility that comes with being a trusted source of information.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted source of environmental and social news in Kenya, setting the standard for journalistic excellence and integrity. We envision a Kenya where every citizen has access to reliable, unbiased information that empowers them to make informed decisions about our environment and society.

We aspire to be a beacon of truth, justice, and service in the media landscape, inspiring a new generation of journalists who are committed to the public good and the preservation of our planet for future generations.

Core Values

Environmental Stewardship

We are committed to protecting and preserving our environment. Our reporting promotes sustainable practices and environmental conservation.

Social Justice

We stand for justice, equity, and fairness in all our reporting. We give voice to the voiceless and advocate for human rights.

Truth & Integrity

We are uncompromising in our commitment to truth and accuracy. Our journalism is built on a foundation of honesty and transparency.

Community Service

We serve our community with dedication and integrity. Our work is driven by a commitment to the public good and social development.

Sustainability

We promote sustainable development and responsible resource use. Our journalism supports a sustainable future for generations to come.

Excellence

We strive for excellence in everything we do. From reporting to design, we aim to be the best in class and set the standard for quality journalism.

Our Leadership Team

Our team is comprised of dedicated professionals who are passionate about environmental and social journalism, committed to serving the community with integrity and excellence. Each member brings unique expertise and a shared commitment to making a difference.
