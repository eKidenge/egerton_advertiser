The Egerton Advertiser

"Truth, Justice & Service to the Nation"

Established 2024

Our Mission

The Egerton Advertiser is committed to delivering accurate, timely, and trustworthy news to the citizens of Egerton and beyond. We believe in the power of journalism to inform, educate, and empower communities. Our mission is to serve as the voice of the people, holding power accountable and giving a platform to the voiceless.

We are dedicated to upholding the highest standards of journalistic integrity, ensuring that every story we tell is fact-checked, balanced, and fair. We serve as the Official Gazette of Egerton, Kenya, carrying the weight of responsibility that comes with that title.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted source of news and information in Kenya, setting the standard for journalistic excellence and integrity. We envision a Kenya where every citizen has access to reliable, unbiased information that empowers them to make informed decisions.

We aspire to be a beacon of truth, justice, and service in the media landscape, inspiring a new generation of journalists who are committed to the public good.
