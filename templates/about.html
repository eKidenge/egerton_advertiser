About The Egerton Advertiser

Our Mission

The Egerton Advertiser is committed to delivering accurate, timely, and trustworthy news to the citizens of Egerton and beyond. We believe in the power of journalism to inform, educate, and empower communities.

To be the most trusted source of news and information in Kenya, setting the standard for journalistic excellence and integrity.
