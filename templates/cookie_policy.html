Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 2024

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us enhance your browsing experience and provide personalized content.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic website functionality
  • Performance Cookies: Help us improve the website
  • Functionality Cookies: Remember your preferences
  • Targeting Cookies: Deliver relevant content and ads
  • Analytics Cookies: Understand how visitors use our site

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
sessionid Maintain user session Session
csrftoken Security and CSRF protection 1 year
_ga Google Analytics tracking 2 years
_gid Google Analytics tracking 24 hours
preferences User preference storage 1 year

4. Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services that set cookies, including Google Analytics, social media platforms, and advertising partners. These services have their own privacy policies.

5. Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies in your browser settings. You can:

  • Delete all cookies
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Receive notifications when cookies are set
  • Manage cookie preferences on a per-site basis

Note: Disabling cookies may affect certain features of our website.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
